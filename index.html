Build a useful piece of the bigger system

Pick a seam. Make it better.

You do not need the private factory to help. Start with a public tool, a reproducible problem or a small synthetic example. Improvements can stand on their own; I can evaluate how they fit the larger system later.

Try a real workflow

Run a public project using its own setup guide. Report your platform, the steps, what you expected and what happened. Accessibility, recovery and confusing first-run experiences are valuable starting points.

Build an adapter or fixture

Suggest a small contract first: inputs, outputs, failure behavior and an offline example. Good places include ticket formats, evidence interchange, hardware discovery, inference queues and synthetic CRM data.

Explore an experiment

Prototype ideas in a bounded branch. Label simulations and proposals, keep the example reproducible, and show what would be needed before integration. Learning projects are welcome here too.

Contribution ideas are invitations to discuss scope, not pre-approved implementation requests. Check each repository’s license and contribution guidance. For private components, use the public atlas issue tracker to propose a generic interface or ask about collaboration.
