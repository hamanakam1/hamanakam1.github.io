A CLEAR STARTING POINT

One story.
Something to try.

The Interactive Demo Sprint gives a software founder or agency one focused browser experience for a product explanation, presentation, or workshop. We agree on scope, fee, and timing before work begins.

01 / DEFINE

One audience and story

Choose the product behavior, workflow, or technical idea your viewer needs to understand.

What we agree
One central interaction and three scenarios with visible expected results.
02 / BUILD

A browser experience

Give someone a useful thing to try, using approved content and demonstration data.

What you receive
A responsive demo with a clear starting state, meaningful interaction, and reset.
03 / HAND OFF

A result you can review

Check the agreed scenarios together and make one consolidated revision.

What you receive
Source files, run instructions, a walkthrough, and the agreed delivery package. See the scope.

WORKING TOGETHER

A small scope you can evaluate.

  1. 01 / Understand

    Identify the owner, recurring problem, current cost or friction, and data boundaries.

  2. 02 / Agree

    Write the deliverable, acceptance checks, fee and access requirements.

  3. 03 / Demonstrate

    Build a focused slice. Show the flow, the failures, and what still needs work.

  4. 04 / Hand off

    Review the result together. Deliver source, setup instructions and a clear next decision.
